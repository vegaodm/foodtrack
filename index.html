Privacy Policy

Your food diary stays on your device.

Spendly Food Track is a local-first food diary. There is no account to create, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. Your meals, photos, voice notes, goals, weigh-ins and food library are stored on your iPhone or iPad, and the developer never receives them.

Effective 7 November 2026 · Version 1.0.1 · iPhone and iPad

At a glance

  • No account. The app works fully offline and never asks for an email address.
  • No tracking. No analytics, advertising identifiers, crash-reporting SDKs or cross-app tracking.
  • No collection. The developer does not receive or store any of your data on a server.
  • No sharing. Nothing leaves the device unless you export a file or explicitly hand a question to an AI service you chose.
  • Purchases through Apple. The app learns only whether Lifetime Membership is owned; it never sees payment details.

What the app stores on your device

Everything below is kept inside the app's sandbox on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere by the app.

You can exclude the app in Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Backups.

Permissions

  • Camera — requested only when you choose to photograph a meal; the picture is saved inside the app.
  • Microphone — requested only when you record a voice note; the recording is saved inside the app.
  • Photo library — the system photo picker is used, so the app receives only the photo you select. It has no access to your library.
  • No location, contacts, calendars, health, motion or notification permissions are requested.

AI lookup

Settings lets you choose where a nutrition question goes. Ask Leo on Brave Search (search.brave.com/ask) is the default; ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, Pocket LLMG, Pocket LLM or a custom URL are also available. When you tap “Open the AI”, the app opens that service — a website in your browser, or an installed app through its own URL scheme — with a question about the food and portion you are entering. The question contains the food name, the grams and the nutrient fields being requested.

Brave Search receives the question when you use the default; Brave's own privacy policy then applies, and Leo's answer is shown in your browser. Pocket LLM and Pocket LLMG, by contrast, are local apps made by the same developer as Spendly Food Track: they run on your device and do not record your questions or any other personal data.

Spendly Food Track itself sends nothing, stores no API key and never receives a reply automatically: you copy the question into any AI, then paste the answer back yourself. If you choose an online service, that service receives the question and its own privacy policy applies; do not put sensitive personal information into a food name or note. If you choose Pocket LLM, Pocket LLMG or another on-device model, the question stays on your device.

Purchases

Lifetime Membership is a one-time, non-consumable purchase processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. Spendly Food Track receives only whether the product is owned. It never sees your name, payment card or Apple Account. Apple's privacy policy applies to the transaction.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in, cloud sync or server of any kind.
  • No analytics, advertising, attribution, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of data with third parties.
  • No background uploads and no network requests for your diary content.
  • No reading of your photo library, contacts, location or health data.

Your control and deletion

  • Edit or delete any entry, photo or recording from the diary.
  • Delete or archive foods in the food library. A food used by past entries is archived instead of deleted, so history keeps its numbers.
  • Use Settings › Delete all data to remove meals, exercise, weigh-ins, photos, recordings and custom foods while keeping the reference library and your goals.
  • Deleting the app removes its sandbox, including the database and media, subject to iOS behaviour.
  • Backups you exported are your files: delete them from Files or iCloud Drive when you no longer need them.

Because the developer holds no personal data, there is nothing on a server to access, correct or erase on your behalf. If you are in the EEA, the UK, California or another region with data-protection rights, those rights are satisfied by the on-device controls above; you can still write to the address below with any question.

Security

App data is protected by the iOS app sandbox and by your device passcode and encryption. The app does not add its own encryption layer. An exported backup is an unencrypted SQLite file, so store it somewhere you trust and delete copies you no longer need.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone. A parent or guardian can remove all data on the device with the controls above.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The effective date at the top always reflects the current version, and material changes are noted in the App Store release notes.

Health notice

Calories, macros and micronutrients — including the bundled reference foods and anything estimated by an AI — are approximations. They are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Consult a qualified professional for dietary or medical decisions.

Contact

KI-Abfrage

In den Einstellungen wählst du, wohin eine Nährwertfrage geht. Standard ist Ask Leo von Brave Search (search.brave.com/ask); außerdem stehen ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, Pocket LLMG, Pocket LLM oder eine eigene URL zur Wahl. Tippst du auf „KI öffnen“, öffnet die App diesen Dienst – eine Website im Browser oder eine installierte App über ihr URL-Schema – mit einer Frage zum eingegebenen Lebensmittel und zur Portion. Die Frage enthält Name, Grammzahl und die gewünschten Nährstofffelder.

Brave Search erhält die Frage, wenn du den Standard nutzt; dann gilt die Datenschutzerklärung von Brave, und Leos Antwort erscheint im Browser. Pocket LLM und Pocket LLMG sind dagegen lokale Apps desselben Entwicklers wie Spendly Food Track: Sie laufen auf deinem Gerät und zeichnen weder deine Fragen noch andere persönliche Daten auf.

Spendly Food Track selbst sendet nichts, speichert keinen API-Schlüssel und erhält keine Antwort automatisch: Du kopierst die Frage in eine beliebige KI und fügst die Antwort selbst ein. Bei einem Online-Dienst erhält dieser die Frage; es gilt seine Datenschutzerklärung. Schreibe keine sensiblen persönlichen Daten in Lebensmittelnamen oder Notizen. Bei Pocket LLM, Pocket LLMG oder einem anderen lokalen Modell bleibt die Frage auf deinem Gerät.

Compras

La membresía de por vida es una compra única y no consumible, procesada íntegramente por Apple mediante StoreKit. Spendly Food Track solo sabe si el producto está comprado. Nunca ve tu nombre, tu tarjeta de pago ni tu ID de Apple. A la transacción se le aplica la política de privacidad de Apple.

Lo que la app no hace

  • Sin cuenta, inicio de sesión, sincronización en la nube ni servidor de ningún tipo.
  • Sin SDK de analíticas, publicidad, atribución, informes de fallos ni seguimiento.
  • Sin vender, alquilar ni compartir datos con terceros.
  • Sin subidas en segundo plano ni peticiones de red para el contenido de tu diario.
  • Sin leer tu fototeca, contactos, ubicación ni datos de salud.

Tu control y la eliminación

  • Edita o elimina cualquier entrada, foto o grabación del diario.
  • Elimina o archiva alimentos de la biblioteca. Un alimento usado por entradas anteriores se archiva en lugar de borrarse, para que el historial conserve sus valores.
  • Usa Ajustes › Eliminar todos los datos para quitar comidas, ejercicio, pesajes, fotos, grabaciones y alimentos propios, manteniendo la biblioteca de referencia y tus objetivos.
  • Eliminar la app borra su espacio, incluida la base de datos y los medios, según el comportamiento de iOS.
  • Las copias de seguridad que exportes son tus archivos: bórralas de Archivos o iCloud Drive cuando ya no las necesites.

Como el desarrollador no guarda datos personales, no hay nada en un servidor que consultar, corregir ni borrar en tu nombre. Si estás en el EEE, el Reino Unido, California u otra región con derechos de protección de datos, los controles del dispositivo anteriores los cubren; aun así puedes escribir a la dirección de abajo con cualquier duda.

Seguridad

Los datos de la app están protegidos por el espacio aislado de iOS y por el código y el cifrado de tu dispositivo. La app no añade su propia capa de cifrado. Una copia de seguridad exportada es un archivo SQLite sin cifrar: guárdala en un lugar de confianza y elimina las copias que ya no necesites.

Menores

La app no está dirigida a menores y no recopila datos personales de nadie. Un padre, madre o tutor puede eliminar todos los datos del dispositivo con los controles anteriores.

Cambios en esta política

Esta política puede actualizarse cuando cambie la app. La fecha de arriba refleja siempre la versión vigente y los cambios importantes se indican en las notas de la versión de la App Store.
